Privacy

We collect the least we can run an honest exchange on: an email address, the tickets, and the transaction. No ad trackers, no selling data, no dark patterns.

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

What we collect

Account: your email address (sign-in is by emailed link — there is no password to store) and an optional display name. Listings: the event, seats, prices, restrictions, and — inside our ticketing account — the tickets themselves once you transfer them. Orders: what was bought, the price breakdown, and the transfer and payout records, including the bank reference for a seller payout. Disputes: what you tell us, who handled it, and the documented outcome (that record-keeping is itself a Code commitment, #8).

What we never do

We don't sell personal data. We don't run advertising trackers or share data with ad networks. We don't take card numbers — payments are handled by our payment processor (Stripe), and card details never touch our servers.

Who sees what

A buyer sees the listing: event, seats, face value, price, restrictions — never the seller's identity or email. A seller sees that their ticket sold and what they're owed — never the buyer's payment details. Ticket transfers move through our account, so buyer and seller don't exchange personal contact details with each other. Service providers we rely on (payment processing, email delivery, hosting, our database) process data only to run the service.

Email

We email you to run the exchange: sign-in links, order and transfer updates, payout notices, and dispute answers. Transactional mail comes from our email provider (Resend). If you joined the founding list on the marketing site, that's kept separate from your account here.

Keeping and deleting

Transaction and dispute records are kept as the audit trail the Code promises — that's how "we hold ourselves to it in public" stays checkable. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, and ask for your account data to be deleted; where a record must be retained for accounting or dispute history, we'll say so plainly rather than quietly keeping it.

Contact

Changed Plans LLC — privacy questions go to the same named humans who answer disputes. Open a dispute from your order page, or email us at seugeneb@comcast.net.
