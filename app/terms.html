Terms of service

Changed Plans LLC operates a fan-to-fan ticket exchange at myplanschanged.com. These terms are the plain-language agreement between you and Changed Plans LLC when you buy or sell here. They are written to match — and never to undercut — the Changed Plans Code, our published code of practice. If these terms and the Code ever conflict, the term more protective of the fan governs.

Last updated: November 3, 2026.

1. What Changed Plans is

Changed Plans is a marketplace where fans pass tickets to other fans. Changed Plans LLC is the merchant of record for every transaction: buyers pay Changed Plans, and Changed Plans pays sellers. We are not the event organizer, the venue, or the primary ticket issuer, and we don't set face values — the venue or issuer printed those on the ticket.

2. Pricing — the rule that never bends

Face value or less, never above. The only extra cost is a 14% buyer fee — and your first transaction is free.

Every listing shows the ticket's face value, and no ticket is ever priced above it — not at listing, not at checkout, not ever (Code #1). The price you see before checkout is the full price you pay: the ticket price plus the 14% buyer fee, with the fee waived entirely on your first completed transaction (Code #2). Sellers receive 100% of the listing price. No fee appears for the first time at the final step.

3. Only tickets we hold

A ticket must be transferred to Changed Plans and verified in our account before it can be listed for sale (Code #4). Nobody sells a ticket they don't yet have — there are no speculative listings. Transferability, entry requirements, and viewing restrictions are stated on every listing before anyone pays (Code #3). Read them: they are part of what you're buying.

4. Buying

Starting checkout holds the listing for 15 minutes while you pay. Your payment is collected by our payment processor into Changed Plans' account and is held — it is not released to the seller — until the ticket transfer to you is verified (Code #5). If the hold lapses unpaid, the order is cancelled and the listing returns for sale; you are not charged for an order that never completed payment.

5. Selling

To sell, you transfer your tickets to the Changed Plans account as instructed at listing; your listing goes live only after we verify them. If you authorize staged price drops, you set three numbers up front — your pocket floor, your game-morning (endgame) floor, and whether we may use the endgame floor without checking with you — and your price never drops below the floor that applies. You are paid 100% of the final listing price after the buyer has the tickets, by manual payment from our business account, with the payment reference recorded on your order.

6. Refunds

Refunds follow our Refund policy, which is part of these terms: a full refund if a ticket sold here doesn't work (Code #6), and at least face value back if an event is cancelled and the organizer authorizes refunds (Code #7).

7. Disputes

Every transaction has a named human on our side. You can open a dispute from your order page; a named person answers within two business days, decides against the published Code, and documents the decision (Code #8). If we got it wrong, the remedy is a refund — not an argument.

8. Fans only

Changed Plans is for fans whose plans changed and fans who'd love to go. No bots, no automated purchasing, no bulk professional resale (Code #9). Accounts are tied to a verified email address; we may close accounts that violate this section.

9. The honest limits

Events are run by organizers, not by us: schedules, lineups, and entry rules can change, and an organizer's own terms also apply to the ticket itself. Our guarantees in the Code and the Refund policy cover what we control — the money and the transfer — and we stand behind them as written there.

10. Changes

If we change these terms, the version in force when you placed your order governs that order. Changes to the Code itself are versioned and published on the site.

11. Governing law

Any dispute that cannot be resolved through the process in section 7 belongs in the state or federal courts located in Massachusetts.
